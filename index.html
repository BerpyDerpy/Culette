02What we're working on

Three projects, from the wall of a rocket engine to an ocean basin.

Machines and robots

Autonomous rover

A tracked robot for ground that is too rough or too risky to send a person across.

The problem
Inspection and handling work on unsafe ground still means sending people into harm's way.
Our solution
A tracked platform with an arm, cameras and a sensor mast, built from six assemblies: drive, hull, arm, mast, sensor head and electronics.
Where it stands
In development, backed by a ₹15 lakh MSME grant.

Engineering software

DESYN

A design tool for the cooling inside 3D-printed rocket engines.

The problem
A rocket engine is limited by heat. Designing the cooling channels inside its wall usually takes weeks of iteration by hand.
Our solution
Software that tests thousands of chamber and channel designs against the physics, then exports the best one as a file ready to 3D-print.
Where it stands
Working, and tested on DLR's published LUMEN engine. It checked 7,200 designs in 12 seconds, and DLR's own design came within 8% of its best result on coolant pressure drop.

Data and AI systems

Ocea

The ocean in 3D, in a web browser.

The problem
Ocean forecasts and float measurements lived in separate, mostly 2D tools, with no quick way to compare one with the other.
Our solution
A 3D ocean showing temperature, salinity and currents through the water column, with real Argo float measurements in the same view.
Where it stands
Working. One view covers 41 ocean regions, and you can step through a 28-step forecast and compare it with float measurements.
