Method

One photograph holds the rule that grew it.

A snow crystal records its own growth. Vapour drifts toward the ice, the tips grow fastest because they reach furthest into it, and the lattice fixes every angle at sixty degrees. Write those facts down as a model and the crystal can be grown again, which is what the top of this page is doing.
