02Computational engineering

DESYN

Preliminary design for regeneratively cooled, 3D-printed LOX/methane thrust chambers. A requirement card goes in; a front of coolable, printable, stable designs comes out in under 30 seconds, every number traceable to its source.

Physics
Thermochemistry, Bartz heat flux, 1-D coolant march, wall stress, acoustic stability screen
Search
NSGA-II over chamber and channel geometry, about 1.6 ms per design
Output
Pareto front, traceable report, checked STL with internal channels
Checks
70 validation cases pass, none fail

Figures are drawn from DESYN's own outputs. LUMEN comparisons are against DLR's design-time CFD, not hot-fire data.
