Generative studies

Six systems, each grown live from a single rule. They follow the six references in the brief. Nothing on this page is an image: every line is computed in your browser as you watch.

Vapour crystal

s′ = u + ½α(ū − u) + v
 

Reiter's automaton. Vapour diffuses across a hexagonal grid and condenses on any cell touching ice. Arms, side branches and six-fold symmetry all come from that one local rule; the faint halo is the vapour the crystal has used up.

Lorenz attractor

ẋ = σ(y − x) ẏ = x(ρ − z) − y ż = xy − βz
 

Three coupled equations from a model of convection. The path never repeats and never leaves its two lobes. The amber point is the present state; the slow turn shows the attractor is a thin surface in three dimensions.

Coaxal envelope

arc through O and Tₖ, tangent angle φⱼ
 

Every line is a circular arc from the centre to one of the tips. Sweeping the arc's tangent angle fills a lens; the outermost arcs draw first, so the silhouette appears before the mesh. Where lenses overlap, their arcs cross into a weave.

Branching grammar

F → FF−[−F+F+F]+[+F−F−F]
 

A Lindenmayer system: a rewriting rule applied a few times, then read as turtle instructions. F steps forward, + and − turn by δ, brackets open and close a branch. Small curl and jitter make it read as grown rather than stamped.

Horn-torus coil

p(t) = ((R + r cos nt) cos t, (R + r cos nt) sin t, r sin nt)
 

A point winds around a torus whose tube radius nearly equals its ring radius, so every loop passes close to the centre. Seen at an angle, the loops overlap everywhere except two dark lenses. A non-integer winding number makes each pass land between the last.

Recursive stellation

apex = centroid + h·n̂, applied twice
 

Each face of a subdivided icosahedron is raised into a pyramid, then many of the pyramid's own faces are raised again. Heights follow a heavy-tailed distribution, so most faces become short crystals and a few become needles.

Reading a plate

The line above each form is its rule. The line below is the live state: step count, parameters, coordinates. Regrow restarts the same system; Vary picks new parameters inside the range where that system stays well-behaved; Expand opens the plate full screen.
